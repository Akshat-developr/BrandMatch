HOW TO READ BRANDMATCH
Evidence first.
Interpretation second.
BrandMatch sends your aggregate cultural signals to Qloo through its official, validated recommend workflow. Returned affinities and explanations are shown as Qloo provides them, with the full response available for review.
Use public entities to represent a group. Never submit names, emails, account identifiers, precise location histories, or other personal data. Affinity is not causation, an individual prediction, or proof of audience identity.
Live discovery requires the event-issued Qloo credential configured privately with qloo setup --qloo.
